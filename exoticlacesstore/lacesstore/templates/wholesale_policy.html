Wholesale Purchase Policy

Last Updated: {% now "F j, Y" %}

1. Wholesale Eligibility

Wholesale purchases are available to:

  • Fabric retailers
  • Boutique owners
  • Fashion designers
  • Tailoring businesses
  • Event planners
  • Resellers

2. Minimum Order Quantities

Minimum order quantities may apply to selected products. Requirements may vary depending on product type, product availability, and manufacturer requirements.

3. Wholesale Pricing

Wholesale pricing is available for qualifying orders. Pricing may depend on quantity purchased, product category, and current market conditions. Please contact us for a custom quotation.

4. Product Availability

Wholesale orders are subject to stock availability. In some cases, products may need to be sourced directly from manufacturers. Additional lead times may apply.

5. Payment Terms

Wholesale orders typically require full payment before shipment. Alternative payment arrangements may be considered for approved business customers.

6. Shipping

Wholesale orders may be shipped through Seller Shipping, DHL Express, freight services, or other logistics partners. Shipping costs are calculated separately unless otherwise agreed.

7. Returns and Refunds

Wholesale purchases are generally non-refundable except where wrong products were supplied, products arrive damaged, or the error originates from Exotic Laces Store.

8. Custom Orders

Custom designs and special orders may require additional production time, advance deposits, and manufacturer approval. Custom orders may not be eligible for cancellation or refund after production begins.

9. International Wholesale Orders

International buyers are responsible for customs duties, import taxes, and regulatory compliance in their destination country.

10. Contact for Wholesale Inquiries

For wholesale quotations and partnership opportunities, please contact Exotic Laces Store with:

  • Desired products
  • Required quantities
  • Destination country
  • Business information

We will provide a customized quotation and shipping estimate.
